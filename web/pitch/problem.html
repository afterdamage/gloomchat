The Problem

Private communication is still treated as a niche, while mass-market tools normalize surveillance, lock-in, and opaque data practices. People who need secure messaging most are often forced to trade usability for safety.

Trust Collapse

  • Centralized messengers monetize user metadata and social graphs.
  • Policy changes can instantly alter privacy guarantees.
  • Users cannot independently verify infrastructure claims.

Adoption Barrier

  • Privacy-first tools are often difficult to onboard and explain.
  • Fragmented workflows across chat, calls, and files hurt retention.
  • Non-technical communities face steep setup and migration costs.

Operational Risk

  • Teams need secure channels across Android, desktop, and web.
  • Reliability and speed cannot be compromised for encryption.
  • Organizations need clear privacy posture for compliance review.
