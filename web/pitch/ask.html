The Ask

We are looking for aligned capital and distribution partners to accelerate secure communication adoption for communities and teams that cannot rely on surveillance-first platforms.

Funding Ask

  • Primary allocation: product engineering, growth, and infrastructure.
  • Outcome target: user growth, conversion to paid hosting, enterprise pilots.

Partnership Ask

  • Launch partners in privacy, journalism, civic tech, and NGO sectors.
  • Pilot cohorts for managed secure comms deployments.
  • Co-marketing opportunities for trust-centered communication tooling.

Immediate Next Step

  • Run a structured pilot with a high-trust, high-need user group.
  • Measure onboarding, engagement, and conversion metrics.
  • Use pilot data to lock fundraising and enterprise roadmap milestones.
