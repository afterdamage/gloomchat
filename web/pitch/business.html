The Business

Our model starts with privacy-conscious communities and scales into recurring revenue through hosted services, enterprise deployments, and strategic partnerships around secure communication infrastructure.

Go-To-Market

  • Early adopters: journalists, activists, open-source communities.
  • Growth channels: app stores, direct community rollout, referrals.
  • Partner-led expansion with privacy organizations and NGOs.

Revenue Streams

  • Hosted premium homeserver subscriptions for teams and power users.
  • Managed enterprise deployments with SLA and support contracts.
  • White-label/community bundles with setup and migration services.

Defensibility

  • Trust + product quality in a market where UX is often weak.
  • Open protocol reduces platform dependency and lock-in risk.
  • Execution moat through compliance-ready release discipline.
